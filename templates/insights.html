HOW TO READ THE GRAPHS

Understanding the live river trend

Each interactive graph combines historical AquaSensor measurements with the latest machine-learning dissolved oxygen forecast. This allows you to compare what has already happened in the river with what the model predicts may happen next.

●
Actual Dissolved Oxygen

The solid blue line represents dissolved oxygen measurements recorded by the AquaSensor in mg/L. These are actual observed river measurements. Moving backwards through the graph allows you to inspect earlier saved readings.

┄
Predicted Dissolved Oxygen

The dashed purple line starts from the latest actual dissolved oxygen reading and continues into the future using machine-learning forecasts for 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90, 105 and 120 minutes.

~
Water Temperature

The orange line represents measured river water temperature. Temperature uses the right-hand vertical axis because it is measured in °C, while dissolved oxygen is measured in mg/L.

Interactive controls

Hover over any point to view the exact date, time and measured value. Use the zoom controls to focus on a shorter period, drag the graph left or right to inspect older or newer readings, and use the range slider beneath the graph to move through the historical record. The reset control returns the graph to its full view.

Past → Present → Future

The Actual DO and Water Temperature lines represent measurements that have already been recorded by the AquaSensor. At the latest measurement, the Predicted DO line begins and extends forward for the next two hours.

Three monitoring stations

AquaPulse provides an individual interactive graph for Derwent 13, Derwent 13-50 and Derwent 21 so that changes in dissolved oxygen and water temperature can be examined separately at each monitoring location.
